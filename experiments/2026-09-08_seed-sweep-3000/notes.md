# Multi-training-seed sweep: seed 3000 (2026-09-08)

Part of the first genuine multi-training-seed sweep on this track (see
`docs/lab_notebook.md`'s 2026-09-08 entry for the full 5-seed comparison
and analysis). Locked-in config identical to the `seed-sweep-909` entry;
seed 3000 is a fresh training seed never used on this track before.

**Result:** tied for fastest lap time in the sweep (17.26s, essentially
matching seed 1000's 17.19s) and 6.20 avg laps, but with the weakest
safety margin of any seed tested: 0.0127 avg damage, 0.22s off-track,
0.15s wall-contact (still small in absolute terms and still 0/20
eliminated, but the highest of the group on every safety metric). Same
speed as seed 1000, worse safety -- seed 1000 is the better pick between
the two. See the lab notebook entry for the full cross-seed table.
