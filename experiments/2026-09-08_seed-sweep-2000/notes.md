Part of the first genuine multi-training-seed sweep on this track (see
`docs/lab_notebook.md`'s 2026-09-08 entry for the full 5-seed comparison
and analysis). Locked-in config identical to the `seed-sweep-909` entry;
seed 2000 is a fresh training seed never used on this track before.

**Result:** the weakest performer in the sweep, though still safe. 0/20
eliminated, 20/20 wins, 0.000 damage, 0.00s off-track/wall-contact
(perfect safety), but only 2.95 avg laps and 34.00s avg best lap time --
the slowest and least complete of any seed tested. Not unstable or
broken (unlike the 2026-09-01 seed-909 freeze), just meaningfully slower
-- see the lab notebook entry for the full cross-seed table and
discussion of the resulting pace variance across training seeds.
